About us

A new export house built around clear paperwork

Digital Ashwin Exports, based in Maharashtra, sells Indian seeds, pulses and nuts to wholesalers, processors and importers, within India and in markets around the world.

We work from a sample and a written specification. Price terms, packing, inspection and payment go into one sales contract, so both sides know what is being shipped before it leaves our warehouse or port.
